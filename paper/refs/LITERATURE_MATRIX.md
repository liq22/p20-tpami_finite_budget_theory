# Literature supporting the coordinate–budget argument

Reading date: 2026-09-18. The 33 core rows below reflect reading of original-paper methods/theory and relevant experimental sections, not a claim to have checked every appendix. Bibliographic links are resolved in `references.bib`; the source column links the original full text actually inspected. Some PDF screenshots were unavailable, so numerical rankings from their figures are not used. Main-text citation keys identify where each source contributes to the argument. Two classical foundations and two abstract-level additional neighbors are listed separately and are **not counted** toward the 33 body-read papers.

The last column states a comparison needed by P20. It is not an assertion that the cited authors never studied a neighboring question.

## Consequence for the gap

Response fidelity, learned transforms, task-driven dictionaries, query efficiency, structural support priors, instancewise selection, and matched confound controls all have direct precedents. P20 must isolate a narrower operational relationship: under the same response law and information, which risk term changes when coordinates or admissible supports change? The same-information union emulation disproves an intrinsic finite-query routing-class advantage. Shared-coordinate learning remains a practical hypothesis requiring matched real-data evidence.
