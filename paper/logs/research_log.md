# Research decision — 2026-09-18

The previous interpretation of a finite-query routing advantage over a static
union was too broad. A static dictionary does not prohibit context-dependent
support selection. With the same information, zero-padding the routed block gives
exact union predictions at any finite Q. The new numerical control confirms
zero prediction discrepancy at all four tested budgets. Shuffling the constructed
context degrades the route. Thus plain-union OMP is a solver comparison, not a
witness of intrinsic routing-class superiority.

The practical hypothesis is narrowed to shared-coordinate learning under matched
response semantics, objective families, support priors and information. The
closest literature already establishes transformed-domain interpretation, learned
wavelets, task-driven dictionaries, structured sparse recovery and confound-aware
selection. No real-data superiority is promoted. The added controls are a
retrospective follow-up to the earlier exploratory result, not a preregistered
confirmation study.
